Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This page explains what information is processed by the tools and projects published on rahimov.tr and its subdomains (e.g. lexirace.rahimov.tr, spythere.rahimov.tr, abyss-diver.rahimov.tr). By using the site you accept this policy.

Information collected

You don't need to create an account or give personal information to use the main site and tools.

  • Preferences stored on your device: your theme and language preference, the city you choose for weather and prayer times, your favourite exchange rates and the gold you add to the gold calculator are kept in your browser's local storage (localStorage). This information is not sent from your device to this site's server.
  • Location: your browser asks for location permission once on your first visit and when you press the “Use my location” button. If you allow it, your approximate coordinates are sent to the relevant data provider only to look up the weather, prayer times and city name. If you don't allow it, Istanbul is shown and permission is not asked again.
  • Account information: in games that need an account, such as LexiRace, the username you choose and your game progress are stored. No email address is requested.
  • Game data: in multiplayer games (e.g. Spythere), the room and game state are kept on the server for as long as the game lasts.
  • Microphone: LexiRace uses the browser's speech recognition for voice answers. Microphone permission is used only during the game; no audio recording is stored by this site.

Third-party data providers

When you open a tool page, your browser requests the data directly from the services below. As with any internet request, your IP address is passed to the service. These services are subject to their own privacy policies.

  • Truncgil Finans, Frankfurter and the open currency archive on jsDelivr (currency and gold), flagcdn (flag images)
  • Open-Meteo (weather and city search), BigDataCloud (finding the city name from your location)
  • The open earthquake service providing Kandilli Observatory data (earthquakes)
  • Aladhan (prayer times)
  • CoinGecko (cryptocurrency prices)
  • Nager.Date (public holidays)
  • Google Fonts (fonts), Google Firebase (game accounts and multiplayer data)
  • Hosting providers (GitHub Pages, Vercel): may keep technical logs when serving pages.

Cookies and Google AdSense ads

Ads may be shown on this site through Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can opt out of third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.

For more on how Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Children's privacy

This site does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think such information has been collected, contact us via the Contact page or at destek.rahimov@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Your rights

Under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), you have the right to access the data held about you and to request its correction or deletion. If you want your account and related data deleted, just get in touch.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always on this page and the date at the top is changed.
