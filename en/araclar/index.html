Tools

Seven small tools for the things people check throughout the day. All free, no sign-up, and easy to use on your phone.

Exchange rates

The dollar, the euro and more than 60 currencies; a converter that works out buying and selling amounts, plus a one-year chart.

Gold prices

Gram, quarter, Republic and five-coin gold prices; work out what all your gold is worth.

Weather

Search your city; current temperature, hourly forecast and 7-day weather.

Recent earthquakes

The latest 100 earthquakes in and around Turkey, from Kandilli Observatory data.

Prayer times

Daily and monthly prayer times for your city, with a countdown to the next one.

Cryptocurrency prices

Dollar and lira prices of Bitcoin, Ethereum and other major cryptocurrencies.

Public holidays

This year's and next year's public holidays, and how many days until the next one.

How do these tools work?

When you open a page, your browser fetches the information directly from open data sources and shows it on screen. There is no sign-up system or server collecting personal data in between. The city and theme you choose are stored only on your own device, so next time the weather and prayer times open straight away with your city.

The price pages (currency, gold, crypto) refresh themselves every two minutes while they stay open. The earthquake list updates at the same interval. If one of the sources stops responding for a while, the page shows a warning saying so and a “Try again” button.

Sources

  • Currency and gold: Truncgil Finans (free-market buying and selling prices), Frankfurter (central bank reference rates, for the charts) and the open currency archive on jsDelivr (charts of other currencies).
  • Weather: Open-Meteo, which uses the numerical weather prediction models of national weather services.
  • Earthquakes: the list published by Boğaziçi University's Kandilli Observatory.
  • Prayer times: Aladhan, using the calculation method of Turkey's Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet).
  • Cryptocurrencies: CoinGecko.
  • Public holidays: Nager.Date.

The information here is for reference only. For money decisions rely on your bank's or jeweller's current price, and in an emergency follow the announcements of AFAD and other official bodies.
