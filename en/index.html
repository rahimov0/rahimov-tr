    Frequently asked questions

    What is this site for?

    It gathers the information you check throughout the day in one place: exchange rates and gold prices, weather, recent earthquakes, prayer times, cryptocurrency prices and public holidays. You can also reach the browser games and apps made by Kanan Rahimov from here.

    Where does the data come from?

    Each tool's page names its source. Exchange rates and gold come from Truncgil Finans and Frankfurter, weather from Open-Meteo, earthquakes from Kandilli Observatory data, prayer times from Aladhan, crypto from CoinGecko and holidays from Nager.Date.

    Are the prices live?

    Currency, gold and crypto prices update every few minutes; this is not tick-by-tick market data. Prices at banks and jewellers may differ from what you see here. Nothing here is investment advice.

    Do I need to sign up or download an app?

    No. All the tools run in your browser and need no sign-up. The city and theme you choose are stored only on your own device.

    Why are there ads on the site?

    Google ads may be shown on some pages. This income covers the domain and hosting costs and helps keep the tools free. Details are on the Privacy Policy page.
